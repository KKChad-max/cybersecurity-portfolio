## Part 2: Identify Three Organizations and Their Objectives

### Organization 1: ISC² (International Information System Security Certification Consortium)

**Mission:** To inspire a safe and secure cyber world. They provide globally recognized credentials, resources, and leadership to advance cybersecurity professionals at every career stage.

**My connection:** I am already a candidate for the **ISC² CC (Certified in Cybersecurity)** certificate. I've completed the prerequisite training and am actively preparing for the exam.

---

### Organization 2: OWASP (Open Worldwide Application Security Project)

**Mission:** To be the global open community that powers secure software through education, tools, and collaboration. Their vision is a world with "No more insecure software."

**Why it fits:** OWASP's free, open-source tools and documentation align with my hands-on, practical approach to learning. Their focus on application security complements my interest in identifying and fixing vulnerabilities.

---

### Organization 3: TryHackMe

**Mission:** A gamified cybersecurity training platform that provides hands-on labs and learning pathways for all skill levels. They aim to make learning cybersecurity engaging and practical through realistic attack and defense scenarios.

**Why it fits:** TryHackMe directly supports my interest in building practical, technical skills through immersive labs, CTF challenges, and a supportive global community. It complements my work on OverTheWire Bandit and reinforces Linux, networking, and security concepts.

## Part 3: Which Organization Aligns With Your Interests and Why

### Organization 1: ISC²

ISC² provides a globally recognized framework and credentials that validate my foundational cybersecurity knowledge. As an active CC candidate, pursuing this certification demonstrates my commitment to professional growth and adherence to industry standards. It's a natural fit for establishing credibility early in my career.

### Organization 2: OWASP

OWASP aligns strongly with my interest in application security and secure coding. Their free resources, tools (like ZAP), and community-driven approach support hands-on learning and real-world vulnerability identification. It's an ideal organization for someone who learns by doing and values open collaboration.

### Organization 3: TryHackMe

TryHackMe is the most directly aligned with my current learning style. It provides exactly the kind of practical, gamified, hands-on experience I've been using to build my Linux, networking, and security skills. The platform's community of learners and CTF-style rooms keep me engaged and continuously challenged.

## Best Choice for My Professional Interests

**OWASP** is the best choice for my current professional interests and career path.

**Why:**
- My journey has been heavily focused on **hands-on, practical learning** (Bandit, TryHackMe, Python scripting, log analysis).
- OWASP provides **free, open-source tools** that I can immediately use to practice and apply my skills.
- Application security is a rapidly growing field, and OWASP offers a direct, community-driven way to stay current.
- My interest in **Python automation** and **vulnerability identification** fits perfectly with OWASP's focus on building and maintaining secure software.
- While ISC² provides the credential I'm working toward, OWASP offers the **practical, day-to-day resources** that will help me grow as a security analyst.
